Refi Radar

Deal sourcing for lenders and brokers: CMBS loans coming due through December 2027, sized for refinancing at current rates.

Loan data: SEC Form ABS-EE, Aug 2026 remittance Rates: Sep 23, 2026 Research write-up

What this is. Refi Radar reads the loan-level data every public CMBS trust files with the SEC each month (Form ABS-EE, Exhibit 102) for 15 conduit deals issued between December 2016 and November 2017. Ten-year loans from those deals are coming due now, so free public data covers this group of refinancings. It keeps loans maturing by Dec 2027 that aren't fully defeased, merges split notes and pari passu pieces, and puts each property in its Census MSA.

How it sizes a refinance. For each lender program on the rate sheet, the maximum new loan is the lowest of three limits: loan-to-value (latest cash flow ÷ cap rate), DSCR at the program's rate and amortization, and minimum debt yield. The cheapest permanent lender that covers the payoff plus closing costs wins. If none does, the tool fills the gap with mezzanine debt up to the mezz CLTV and then preferred equity. Every number is editable under Edit assumptions.

  • Outcomes: Cash-out refi (a permanent lender covers the payoff with 10%+ to spare) · Refi-ready · Recap: mezz · Recap: mezz + pref · Equity gap (the stack can't reach the payoff) · Workout (delinquent or with the special servicer).
  • Opportunity score (0 to 100) weights loan size at 45%, time to maturity at 25% (loans 3 to 12 months out score highest) and how likely the refinance is to work at 30%. Lenders can sort by maturity, balance or debt yield instead.
  • Limits: servicer financials can be 6 to 12 months old or older; partial-year figures are annualized. For pari passu loans, the whole-loan balance is estimated from reported debt service. Borrower and sponsor names aren't in ABS-EE, so featured loans cite the deal prospectus. Market rates are indicative, not quotes.
